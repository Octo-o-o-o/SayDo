SayDo Privacy Policy

Effective: August 21, 2026 | Developer: Yixiao Wang (individual) | Contact: support@octoooo.com

The one-sentence version

SayDo runs no servers. Conversations, items, and ledger data are stored on your own devices (your computer and your phone). Product data travels on your own LAN or an encrypted overlay you configure. Cloud voice, AI upstreams, and push that you enable yourself are called directly from your computer to those third parties. The developer cannot access — and does not collect — any of your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no developer-operated backend, no account system, no analytics, and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs. No user data is uploaded to the developer. AI, optional cloud voice, and optional push that you configure yourself are governed by your agreements with those third parties.

Device capabilities the app uses, and why

Speech recognitionTranscribing your speech to text (uses the system speech recognizer)Transcribed text is sent only to your own paired computer; see the microphone row above for the network-recognition exception
Local networkCommunicating with the SayDo service on your own computerYour LAN or an encrypted overlay you configure, authenticated with the pairing token

Storage and transmission

  • All items, conversations, and ledger data live in the SayDo data directory on your computer; the phone acts only as a display and input device for your computer;
  • Phone-to-computer communication happens on your LAN or an encrypted overlay you configure, authenticated by a token exchanged via QR pairing; the token is kept in the phone's secure system storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore / the HarmonyOS equivalent);
  • Third-party AI, optional cloud voice, and optional push that you enable on the desktop side are called directly by your computer, under your own agreements with those providers; the developer of this app never touches or sees them.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all phone-side local data (pairing information and caches). Desktop-side data is managed and deleted by you on your computer.

Children

The app is not designed for children under 14, collects no personal information, and contains no content directed at children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
